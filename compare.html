QVR Watch records with the watch's own camera

Most watch camera apps turn the watch into a remote for the phone's camera. QVR Watch does the opposite: the watch records, and the phone is the remote.

What QVR Watch does not do

It has no live view, no photos, and no copying of videos to the phone. It needs a full Android watch with a camera.
