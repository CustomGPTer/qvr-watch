QVR Watch 1.0.0, first test build. Two apps:

- QVR-Watch.apk goes on a full Android watch with a camera. It records video with sound, saved on the watch.
- QVR-Watch-Control.apk goes on your phone. It starts and stops recording on the watch over Bluetooth, and changes the watch's recording settings.

Both need Android 10 or later and Bluetooth Classic. They are not for Wear OS-only, Apple, Huawei or fitness watches. Neither app uses the internet, an account or the cloud.

This build has not yet been tried on a real watch.
